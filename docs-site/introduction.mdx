The Flizy Pay API lets a platform do two things:

- **Pay out.** Send ETH, FLZ or tUSD from your platform's wallet to a person: a Flizy username or a wallet address is paid directly; a phone number, an email or a Telegram, Discord or GitHub id gets a claim link they collect with.
- **Collect.** Create a checkout for an amount, send your customer to its page, and they pay from any wallet or from their Flizy account, straight to your platform's wallet.

Flizy never holds your money or your customers'. Payouts leave your own wallet, and only through a daily allowance you sign with your passkey, so the chain itself caps what the API can send. Checkout payments go from the customer's wallet to yours in one transaction.

## How it fits together

1. Flizy sets up your platform and its approved wallet.
2. You create keys on the Developers page of your Flizy account (Account, Developers).
3. Your server calls `https://flizy.app/api/v1` with a key.
4. Flizy tells your server about every change through webhooks, and you can read any payout or payment at any time.

## Conventions

- Amounts are decimal **strings** in the asset's unit, never numbers: `"12.5"`, not `12.5`.
- Times are ISO 8601 in UTC.
- Every answer carries an `X-Request-Id` header. Quote it when you contact Flizy about a call.
- `accepted` means Flizy took the request. It is never a completed payment: only `confirmed` and `refunded` are, and `completed` on every object says so.
