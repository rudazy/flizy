---
title: "Rate limits"
description: "How many calls a key may make."
---

Each key may make **120 calls a minute**. Over that, calls get `429` with code `rate_limited` until the minute has passed. Every call that gets past authentication counts, including ones answered `400` or `404`; a `429` itself does not.

- Spread bursts out, and retry a `429` after a short wait with some random jitter.
- Use webhooks instead of polling. To catch up after downtime, list payouts or payments with `limit=100` and page with `before`.
- Different keys have separate limits, but they all act for the same platform and share its payout caps.

## Payout caps

Separate from the rate limit, Flizy sets for each asset your platform may pay out:

- **The most one payout may move.** Over it, the create is refused with `400` `over_max_payout`.
- **The most per day.** Payouts spend only through the allowance you sign with your passkey, for that daily amount, and the chain enforces it. A payout over what is left today fails with `error_code` `allowance_exceeded` (or `over_daily_max`), and nothing is sent another way.

An asset Flizy has not enabled for your platform is refused with `400` `asset_not_enabled`.
